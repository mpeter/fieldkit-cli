# Support

fieldkit is maintained as an open-source side project. Community support is best-effort: there is no
commercial support contract, guaranteed response time, or promise that every integration or feature
request will be accepted.

## Before asking

1. Check the [documentation](docs/index.md) and
   [troubleshooting reference](docs/reference/troubleshooting.md).
2. Search existing issues for the exact command, error, or integration.
3. Run `fieldkit --version` and the relevant `fieldkit doctor` command.
4. Remove credentials, session cookies, customer names, email content, private hosts, and absolute
   home paths from anything you plan to post.

## Choose the right route

- **Usage or setup question:** use the support-question issue form.
- **Reproducible unexpected behavior:** use the bug form.
- **New capability or behavior change:** use the feature-proposal form.
- **Documentation problem:** use the documentation form.
- **Suspected vulnerability:** follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); never post exploit details publicly.
- **Community moderation concern:** use GitHub's report or block controls. The sole maintainer applies
  the contribution and platform rules.

For public questions, provide the smallest reproducible example and your fieldkit, Python, and
operating-system versions. Maintainers may close requests that cannot be reproduced, expose private
data, fall outside the documented compatibility boundary, or require access to an organization the
project cannot provide.

Consulting, private deployment assistance, and organization-specific service operation are outside
the community support commitment.

# Account Deep Dive — Output Template

Use this format when the user requests a deep dive or research pass on a single account.

```markdown
# Account Research — [Account Name]
Date: YYYY-MM-DD

## Executive Summary

[3–4 sentences: current relationship state, biggest risk, biggest opportunity]

---

## Company Snapshot

**Industry:** [industry]
**Revenue:** [if public, else DATA NEEDED]
**Employees:** [approximate]
**Key Priorities:** [what the company is publicly focused on — from earnings, press, strategy docs]
**Recent News:** [2–3 material items with dates and sources]

---

## Account Health

**Overall Status:** [Strong / At Risk / Declining / Growing]
**Engagement Trend:** [Increasing / Stable / Declining — from Backstory activity data]
**Last Executive Touch:** [date, who, context]
**Open Pipeline:** [count of opportunities from CY26 Salesforce Pipeline section]
**Active Risks:** [count of HIGH severity risks from Active Risks section]
**Open Revenue:** [ARR if known, else DATA NEEDED]

---

## Stakeholder Intelligence

### New or Changed Contacts
| Name | Title | Change | Implication |
|------|-------|--------|-------------|
| [name] | [title] | [new hire / promoted / departed] | [what this means for us] |

### Coverage Assessment
- **Economic Buyer access:** [Strong / Weak / None]
- **Champion strength:** [name + assessment]
- **Single-thread risk:** [yes/no + detail]
- **Gaps to close:** [list]

---

## Trigger Events

| Event | Date | Source | Opportunity / Risk |
|-------|------|--------|--------------------|
| [event] | [date] | [source] | [how to act on it] |

---

## Competitive Landscape

**Known competitors in account:** [names]
**Recent competitive activity:** [signals from Backstory, web, or internal channels]
**Our position:** [where we're strong, where we're vulnerable]

---

## Expansion Opportunities

| Opportunity | Customer Signal | Bridge from Current Work | Readiness |
|-------------|----------------|--------------------------|-----------|
| [opp] | [what they said/did] | [connection] | [Ready / 1-2Q / Longer] |

---

## Risk Register

| Risk | Severity | Evidence | Mitigation |
|------|----------|----------|------------|
| [risk] | [High/Med/Low] | [what we see] | [action] |

---

## Recommended Actions

1. **This week:** [specific action with named stakeholder]
2. **This month:** [strategic move]
3. **This quarter:** [positioning play]
```

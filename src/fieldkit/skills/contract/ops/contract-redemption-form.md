# Consulting-unit redemption draft

Prepare a draft redemption document only when the operator supplies the applicable
agreement, approved template, and current conversion rates. fieldkit does not ship
commercial rates or a standard redemption agreement.

## Required inputs

Obtain the approved template and its revision, customer identity, scope, role
quantities and units, term dates, applicable rate table, available credit balance,
and any authorized expense-conversion rule. Ask for missing quote references,
expiry dates, and opportunity identifiers; never invent them.

A previous engagement's document is not automatically an approved current
template. Confirm which terms and sections remain applicable before reusing it.

## Prepare and calculate

Read the supplied template and scope. Identify editable fields, protected legal
text, signature blocks, tables, and layout requirements from that document rather
than assuming exhibit numbers or section positions.

For each role, multiply its approved units-per-hour rate by the supplied hours.
Clarify whether hours are per person or a total before applying staffing quantity;
do not count it twice. Add only compatible unit totals.

Convert expenses only when the agreement explicitly permits it and supplies a
positive currency-per-unit rate. Use matching currencies, authorized rounding,
and the applicable balance/expiry rules. If a required rate or rule is missing,
leave the calculation unresolved and report the missing input.

## Create a draft

Use an available, authorized document-editing tool that can preserve the supplied
template. No external skill or particular home-directory layout is guaranteed by
fieldkit. If the necessary tooling is unavailable, report that dependency instead
of claiming a document was generated.

Preserve approved legal text and signature blocks. Populate only authorized
fields. Keep the source template unchanged and write a distinctly named draft
inside the configured workspace only after confirming the destination with the
operator. Do not overwrite an existing draft without approval.

## Verify and hand off

Recompute totals independently from the supplied inputs. Check billing units,
currency, quantities, dates, references, balances, and unresolved placeholders.
Use the available document tool's structural validation and reopen or render the
output to inspect layout, tables, pagination, and preserved boilerplate. File
existence alone is not validation.

Report the draft location, source revisions, calculation assumptions, validation
results, and unresolved fields. If validation could not run, say so explicitly.
Do not present the draft as executed, legally approved, or ready to send.
Submission, signatures, CRM updates, and external delivery require separate
operator authorization.
